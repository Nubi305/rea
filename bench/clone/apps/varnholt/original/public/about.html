About

Varnholt keeps a small courier's van loads in order.

A manifest is one van load bound for one destination. Parcels are booked onto an open manifest, the manifest is sealed when the van is packed, dispatched when it leaves and delivered when it comes back empty.

How charges work

Every parcel pays a handling fee plus a rate per kilogram. Fragile parcels pay a surcharge.
